Why hand-coded

Motion is not decoration.
It is information.

Good animation is invisible. It tells the eye where to go next, ranks what matters, and confirms that something just happened. I design the rhythm first — what enters, what waits, what leaves — and only then write it, frame by frame. The result is a page people move through without ever hesitating.
